# Setup details not in the paper

- **Runs:** each patient is interviewed eight times by Qwen 4B and Gemma 4B and twice by each larger model, at temperature 0.8, with replies of at most 40 tokens. Thinking is disabled (`enable_thinking: false` for the local models and Qwen3.8-Flash; `thinking: disabled` for DS-V4.1-Flash).
- **GLM-5.3-Flash:** this model cannot disable thinking, so it runs at its lowest setting (`reasoning_effort: low`), with room for its reasoning tokens. Per condition, 0.9-17.0% of its calls contain thinking, and no reply exceeds 40 tokens.
- **Statistics:** per-patient mean accuracy, also averaged over the models in each group (Small mean, Larger mean); patient-paired bootstrap with 10,000 resamples (seed 0). When the statistician diagnoses from random questions, it uses the same number of random menu questions as the LLM asked, averaged over five seeds.
- **Statistician:** each condition's answer distribution for every question is Laplace-smoothed.
- **Fine-tuning:** LoRA with rank 16, alpha 32, learning rate 1e-4, one epoch. The training data are the statistician's own interviews (B=10) of the labeled patients, with leave-one-out statistics and four interviews per patient, 9,634 turns in all, each ending with the true diagnosis. The two training sets differ only in whether the advice line is in the input.
- **Fine-tuned Qwen 4B:** allowed 15 questions, it never asks more than 10, so it is compared with the statistician at B=10. With the advice line it reaches 70.4% (+1.4 [-2.2, +5.0]). Without the line in the input it reaches 54.0% (-15.0 [-20.5, -9.6]).
- **Unparsable replies:** an interview without a parsable diagnosis counts as wrong. Without advice, this affects Gemma 4B in 74 of 2,400 interviews at B=10 (0.73 unparsable replies per interview) and 43 of 2,400 at B=15 (0.45 per interview). At B=10 this affects its accuracy by at most 3.1 points.
- **Conditions without labeled patients:** two conditions have no labeled patients. Only one appears in the evaluation set: Ebola, 6 of 300 patients, all misdiagnosed by the statistician. Without them, the statistician reaches 70.4% at B=10 and 80.3% at B=15. Every comparison is repeated without them in `robustness_note.pdf`.
